Looking for free WiFi?
You just scanned a random QR code.

Rest assured: your device has not been compromised. This is an educational demonstration organized by GMSA-KNUST IT & Media to build awareness around physical QR code vulnerabilities.

No personal data collected
Threat Mechanism

Anatomy of a Quishing Vector

01

The Physical Lure

Contextual

Attacker places an enticing "KNUST Free WiFi" sticker or decal in high-traffic campus zones like lecture halls, bus stops, and cafeteria tables.

02

The Blind Scan

Obfuscation

Unlike visible text links, physical QR codes fully obscure the destination URL until your mobile device camera or scanner parses and processes the matrix.

03

The Threat Handshake

Execution

One reflex tap redirects your browser directly to an unwetted endpoint, triggering credential harvesters, rogue profiles, or malicious script execution.

Risk Assessment

What Could Have Happened

Fake Login Portal

Critical

A replicated KNUST student AIM/portal intercepting student index numbers, institutional emails, and master passwords.

Malicious Payload

High

Silent background downloads of trojan APK packages or malicious iOS configuration profiles compromising local storage and contacts.

Mobile Money Fraud

Severe

Deceptive UI simulating Ghana MoMo (MTN, Telecel, AT) verification dialogs prompting USSD pin approval or instant transfer authorize requests.

Standard Operating Procedure

How to Scan Safely

5 Rules
Rule 01

Inspect physical label integrity

Look for raised edges, peeling vinyl, or secondary stickers slapped directly over authentic university signage.

Rule 02

Examine the full URL domain

Scrutinize preview links before tapping. Ensure official pages terminate strictly with .edu.gh or knust.edu.gh.

Rule 03

Never login via QR redirects

Do not enter campus portal credentials on any destination page loaded straight from a scanned poster.

Rule 04

Abort surprise download prompts

Authentic public networks never demand manual APK, certificate, or configuration profile installation.

Rule 05

Verify via official channels

Confirm legitimate WiFi access protocols directly via the University Information Technology Services (UITS) portal.

Campus Security Axiom

“Think Before You Click.”

Spread cybersecurity awareness across halls, hostels, and study hubs. One alert student protects hundreds from coordinated digital theft.
